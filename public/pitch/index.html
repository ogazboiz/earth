How the exchange works07 / 11

List. Verify. Trade.

01

List

A farm, a mine, a mill or a cooperative lists what it has: the product, the origin, the volume.

02

Verify

Quality, quantity and documents are checked before anything moves. Nobody trades with a ghost.

03

Trade

Buyers find the listing, agree terms on the exchange, and the deal is documented end to end.

SayThree steps. The seller lists. We verify, so a gold mine on Earth is a real gold mine. Then the buyer finds it and they trade. Verification is what makes people trust it.
